In BDSM, or power exchange, relationships, there is often a dominant person(s) and a submissive person(s). The experiences can be very intense and intimate, as the participants deepen trust and closeness, and the ability to go into a deep headspace that those of us in the life call topspace and subspace. Those two experiences are very different with often widely different intellectual and emotional states. People learn things about themselves that might be shocking or embarassing or shaming, or maybe exultant, jubilant, sublime, sometimes all of these things at the same time.

In this series, I imagine what a submissive might be experiencing inwardly, brought out.
